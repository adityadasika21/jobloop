LLM Engineer (Remote) at Joveo AI

About the role
We are looking for an LLM Engineer to build and scale production LLM
applications powering our recruitment marketing platform.

Requirements
- 3+ years of software engineering experience, with at least 2 years building
  production machine learning or LLM-powered systems
- Strong Python skills and experience with FastAPI or similar frameworks
- Hands-on experience building RAG pipelines, including vector search and
  embeddings (FAISS, Pinecone, or similar)
- Experience with agentic frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, or DSPy
- Experience with LLM evaluation and observability — building eval harnesses,
  LLM-as-judge rubrics, tracing and monitoring in production
- Familiarity with prompt engineering, structured output, and function calling
- Experience deploying and serving models (vLLM, TGI, or hosted APIs)
- Comfortable with Docker and cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP)

Nice to have
- Experience fine-tuning open-source models (LoRA, QLoRA)
- Knowledge graph or Neo4j experience
- Experience in HR tech or recruitment domain
- Kubernetes experience

What you will do
- Design and ship multi-agent workflows and tool-calling systems
- Own model quality: build evaluation pipelines that gate releases
- Reduce inference cost and latency across the serving stack
