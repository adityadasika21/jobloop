Why Join Us?
AI is transforming our platform, our customer experience, and the future of HR technology; this role will be at the forefront of turning that vision into reality. About the role
The Forward Deployed Engineer embeds with Darwinbox's most strategic enterprise customers to ship production-grade AI agents and Agentic Flows on the Darwinbox HCM platform inside each customer's environment. The role centres on three skill areas: production Python engineering for AI agents, deployment of LLM applications (prompt engineering at scale, RAG, agent orchestration, evaluation frameworks), and architecting REST API and webhook integrations with payroll, ATS, SSO/IDP, learning, and ERP systems. You will work hands-on inside customer environments on HR workflow problems that exceed the platform's standard configuration surfaces.

What you will do
- Embed with 2–3 strategic enterprise customers in parallel as the technical owner of the Darwinbox deployment in their environment.
- Run discovery with CHROs, HRBPs, and HRIS leads to surface the real problem behind the stated ask and translate it into a working technical design.
- Deliver a working prototype within ~1 week of engagement kick-off, codify repeatable deployment patterns, and channel field insights into the Darwinbox platform roadmap.
- Design and ship production-grade AI agents on the Darwinbox platform for HR workflows including leave, performance, attendance, onboarding/exit automation, and HR decision support.
- Architect custom integrations between Darwinbox and customer-side payroll, ATS, SSO/IDP, learning platforms, and ERP stacks using REST APIs, webhooks, and event-driven patterns.
- Travel 25–40 percent of the time to customer sites across India, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East as required.
